What happens after you send this

A coordinator reads the request, checks which staff member fits your pet, and emails two or three confirmed time options. If the service needs vaccine records we will ask then, or request them from your previous clinic with your written permission. You confirm one of the times, and only then is anything booked.

Cancelling is free with 24 hours notice.

Inside 24 hours we charge 50 percent of the service. Holiday boarding needs 7 days. Full detail in the cancellation and refund policy.

Before your first appointment

What should I bring?

Vaccine records if you have them, any medication in its original packaging, and the food your pet normally eats if they are staying with us overnight. Bring a leash or a carrier your pet is used to rather than a new one.

How early should I arrive?

Ten minutes is enough for a first visit, five after that. If your pet is anxious in waiting rooms, call from the parking lot and we will bring you straight through.

Can I stay with my pet during the appointment?

For veterinary exams, yes, and we prefer it. For grooming and daycare assessments we usually ask owners to leave, because most animals settle faster once the person they are looking to for reassurance is out of the room.

Do you take walk-ins?

For nail trims, yes, at any location during opening hours. Everything else needs an appointment, though we hold same-day sick slots open at every location until 3 PM on weekdays.
