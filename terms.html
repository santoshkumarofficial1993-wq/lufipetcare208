1. The agreement

These terms form a binding agreement between you and Harbor & Hound Pet Care, LLC. By using this website, submitting a form, or booking any service, you accept them. If you do not accept them, please do not use the site or book with us.

You must be at least 18 years old and legally able to enter a contract. You confirm that you are the owner of the animal you present to us, or that you have the owner's authority to make decisions about that animal's care.

2. Our services

We provide veterinary wellness care, grooming, overnight boarding, daycare, dog walking, cat drop-in visits, and behavior training, at four locations in Colorado. Details and rates are published on the services and pricing pages.

We are not a 24 hour emergency veterinary hospital. We do not provide overnight veterinary cover, specialist surgery, oncology, or after-hours emergency treatment. Where your animal needs care beyond our scope, we will tell you and refer you.

We are not an insurer, an insurance broker, or a lender, and we do not sell insurance, credit, or financing products.

3. Bookings

Submitting a booking form is a request, not a confirmed appointment. A booking exists only once we send you a written confirmation naming the service, date, time and location.

Quoted prices are valid for 30 days. Once you have a written confirmation, we honour the price quoted in it even if our published rates change before your appointment.

Cancellations, changes, no-shows and refunds are governed by the cancellation and refund policy, which forms part of these terms.

4. Your obligations

You agree to:

  • Give us accurate and complete information about your animal's health, medications, allergies, diet and behavior, including any history of biting, resource guarding, escaping, or aggression toward people or other animals.
  • Provide current proof of vaccination as set out in our FAQ, at least seven days before boarding, daycare or a training class.
  • Tell us immediately if your animal develops a contagious condition, including kennel cough, parvovirus, ringworm, fleas or intestinal parasites.
  • Keep your contact details and emergency contact current, and be reachable during any period your animal is in our care.
  • Collect your animal at the agreed time, or tell us as soon as you know you cannot.
  • Pay for services as set out in section 7.
  • Treat our staff with respect. We may end this agreement immediately for abusive, threatening or discriminatory behavior toward any member of our team.

Withholding a known history of aggression is a serious breach. If an undisclosed history contributes to an injury to a person or another animal, you may be liable for the resulting loss, and our liability is excluded to the extent permitted by law.

5. Veterinary consent while your animal is in our care

By leaving your animal with us for boarding, daycare or a walk, you authorise us to seek veterinary treatment if we reasonably believe it is needed and we cannot reach you.

Your boarding or daycare agreement sets a spending limit that you choose in advance. We will not exceed that limit without your consent, except where a veterinarian judges that immediate treatment is necessary to prevent death or serious suffering. You are responsible for the cost of veterinary treatment your animal receives, including treatment provided by a third party emergency hospital.

6. When we say no

We may decline or stop a service where:

  • An animal is showing signs of a contagious illness.
  • Required vaccinations are not current.
  • An animal is distressed to a degree that continuing would be unsafe or unkind. In that case we stop, tell you what happened, and charge only for the part completed.
  • Continuing would put a person or another animal at risk.
  • We cannot meet our published staffing ratios that day.
  • An account has an unpaid balance more than 30 days overdue.

7. Billing

Payment is due when the service is delivered, unless we have agreed otherwise in writing. We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover, cash, and CareCredit where you hold an existing account.

Veterinary work expected to exceed $250 receives a written estimate with a low and a high figure. We will not exceed the high figure without contacting you first.

Memberships bill monthly on the same date to the card on file, may be paused once per year for up to 60 days, and may be cancelled with 30 days written notice. There is no enrolment fee, no cancellation fee, and no minimum term. Membership benefits stop on the cancellation date and unused benefits do not carry over or convert to cash.

Published rates may change. We post any change on the pricing page at least 30 days in advance and email active clients and members.

Balances unpaid after 30 days may attract interest at 1 percent per month, or the maximum rate permitted by Colorado law if lower. We may suspend future bookings while a balance is overdue.

Boarding: if an animal is not collected within seven days of the agreed collection date and we cannot reach you or your emergency contact, we will follow Colorado law regarding abandoned animals. We would much rather talk to you, so please call us if circumstances change.

Never delay seeking veterinary care because of something you read here. Outcomes vary between animals and we do not guarantee any clinical or behavioural result. Training outcomes in particular depend on work done between sessions and on factors outside our control.
