Questions we get most weeks

Do you handle emergencies?

We hold same-day sick slots at every location until 3 PM on weekdays, and our veterinarians handle urgent but stable cases. We are not a 24 hour emergency hospital. Outside our hours, or for anything critical, call VRCC Veterinary Specialty and Emergency Hospital or your nearest emergency veterinary hospital immediately.

What vaccines do you require for boarding and daycare?

Dogs need rabies, DHPP, and Bordetella given at least seven days before arrival. Cats need rabies and FVRCP. We ask for canine influenza where a client has travelled recently. If we hold your records already you do not need to send anything.

Can I keep my current veterinarian?

Yes. Plenty of clients use us for grooming, daycare, boarding or training while staying with their own clinic. We will request records with your written permission so our staff know about allergies and medications, and we send visit notes back to your clinic on request.

What is your cancellation window?

Cancel or move an appointment more than 24 hours ahead at no charge. Inside 24 hours we charge 50 percent of the service. Boarding over a holiday period needs 7 days. Full detail is in the cancellation and refund policy.

Do you take pet insurance?

We are not an insurer and we do not sell insurance. We bill you directly and provide an itemised invoice with diagnostic codes that every major US pet insurer accepts for reimbursement. We can also submit paperwork on your behalf for Trupanion and Nationwide.

My dog is reactive or anxious. Is that a problem?

No, and it is worth telling us in the intake form. We schedule reactive dogs into quiet hours, use separate entrances at three of our four sites, and our staff are Fear Free certified. If a dog is not coping we stop, tell you honestly, and adjust the plan.
