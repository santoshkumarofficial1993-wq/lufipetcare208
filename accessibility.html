Accessibility statement

What we have built, what still needs work, and how to reach us if something on this site or in one of our buildings blocks you.

1. Our commitment

People book pet care from phones on buses, from screen readers, with tremors, with low vision, and with limited patience for a website that fights them. We build for that, and when we fall short we would rather hear about it than not know.

Accessibility work here is continuous rather than a one-off audit. Every new page is checked before it goes live, and we review the whole site twice a year.

2. The standard we work to

We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines version 2.2 at Level AA. We assess ourselves as partially conformant, which means most of the site meets the standard and the exceptions are listed in section 4 rather than hidden.

Testing is done with keyboard only navigation, with NVDA on Windows and VoiceOver on macOS and iOS, at 200 percent browser zoom, and with automated checks in the browser. Automated tools catch perhaps a third of real problems, so manual testing carries the rest.

3. What we have done

  • Every page has one skip link to the main content as the first focusable element.
  • All functionality works with a keyboard alone, with a visible focus indicator that meets the 3:1 contrast requirement.
  • Body text meets or exceeds 4.5:1 contrast against its background, and large text meets 3:1. Placeholder text meets the same body text ratio rather than being pale grey.
  • Text can be zoomed to 200 percent without loss of content or horizontal scrolling. Pinch zoom is not disabled.
  • Every form input has a visible label above it, not a placeholder standing in for one. Errors appear next to the field they belong to, in text as well as colour, and are announced to screen readers.
  • Images that carry meaning have descriptive alternative text. Decorative images have empty alternative text so screen readers skip them.
  • Headings run in a correct order without skipped levels, and landmarks mark the header, navigation, main and footer.
  • All animation and scroll effects stop entirely when your system is set to reduce motion. The site is fully usable in that mode.
  • Content is readable if transparency effects are unavailable, and the site falls back to solid backgrounds when your system asks for reduced transparency.
  • Touch targets are at least 44 by 44 pixels with spacing between them.
  • Nothing on the site relies on colour alone to convey meaning.
  • The site works without JavaScript for reading, though forms need JavaScript to validate before sending.

4. Known limitations

We would rather tell you than let you discover these.

  • Third party booking portal. The owner portal we use for records and appointment history is supplied by a vendor and we do not control its markup. Parts of it fall short of AA. We have raised specific issues with the vendor and we will do anything in the portal for you by phone in the meantime.
  • Older guide content. A small number of guides published before 2024 have alternative text that describes the image without explaining why it is there. We are rewriting these, expected complete by December 2026.
  • Map links. Directions open in Google Maps, which we do not control. Every location also has its full street address written out on the contact page so you never need the map.
  • PDF invoices. Invoices are generated as PDFs that are not tagged for screen readers. Ask and we will send the same information as plain text or as an accessible email.

5. Access at our buildings

  • Denver, LoDo. Step-free entrance from Blake Street. Elevator to Suite 210. Accessible restroom. Two designated accessible parking spaces in the Wazee Street lot.
  • Boulder. Step-free entrance from the rear lot on 23rd Street. The Pearl Street front entrance has one step. Accessible restroom.
  • Fort Collins. Step-free entrance from the Mason Street sidewalk. Accessible restroom. Widest interior circulation of our four sites.
  • Colorado Springs. Step-free entrance on the north side. Accessible restroom. The cat wing is reached through a 32 inch doorway.

Every location welcomes service animals. If you need a quiet appointment time, help getting your pet from the car, or a member of staff to meet you at the door, ask when you book and we will arrange it at no cost.

6. Alternative ways to reach us

You never have to use this website to be a client. Call (720) 431-0688 and a coordinator will book, quote, take payment and answer questions on the phone. We can send confirmations and visit notes by text message instead of email if that suits you better. Spanish speaking staff are available on weekdays.

7. Report a barrier

If something here stopped you, please tell us. Email hello@harborandhound.com with the word accessibility in the subject line, or call (720) 431-0688.

Tell us the page, what you were trying to do, and what happened. Your browser and any assistive technology you use helps us reproduce it, but do not worry if you do not know those details.

We acknowledge every report within two business days and give you a fix date, or an explanation and a workaround if a fix will take longer. Reports about the site go to our web team; reports about a building go to the location manager and to our operations lead.
