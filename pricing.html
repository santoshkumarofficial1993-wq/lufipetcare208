What a membership does not cover.

Surgery, anaesthesia beyond the dental credit, prescription medication, diagnostic imaging, specialist referral, emergency care, and any service listed below at its own rate. Those are billed separately and quoted before we begin.

How billing works.

Memberships bill on the same date each month to the card on file. You can pause once per year for up to 60 days, and cancel any time with 30 days notice. We do not charge a cancellation fee and we do not lock you into a term.

Payment, estimates and what we never do

Written estimates before treatment.

Any veterinary work over $250 gets a written estimate with a low and high figure. If the cost is going to exceed the high figure, we stop and call you. We do not proceed on a verbal nod.

Accepted payment methods.

Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover, cash, and CareCredit where you already hold an account. We do not issue credit ourselves and we do not offer buy now pay later financing.

Insurance reimbursement.

We are not an insurer. We bill you directly and give you an itemised invoice that every major US pet insurer accepts. We can submit claim paperwork for Trupanion and Nationwide at no charge.

Fees we do not charge.

No booking fee, no card processing fee, no fuel surcharge, no fee to administer medication, no fee to email records to another clinic, and no enrollment fee to start a membership.

Prices can change, and we tell you first.

Rates on this page were last reviewed on 1 June 2026. When a rate changes we post it here at least 30 days beforehand and email active clients and members. Any appointment already booked is honoured at the price you were quoted. Read the full billing terms in our terms of service and the cancellation and refund policy.
