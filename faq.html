Frequently asked questions

If your question is not here, call (720) 431-0688 or send a message. A coordinator answers, not a bot.

Appointments and bookings

How do I book?

Use the booking form or call us. The form is a request, not a confirmed slot. A coordinator replies within one business day with two or three confirmed times, and the appointment exists once you pick one and we email a confirmation.

Is the first visit really free?

Yes. The intro visit is twenty minutes, involves no examination and no handling your pet does not want, and costs nothing. There is no obligation to book anything afterwards and we do not ask for card details to arrange it.

Do you take walk-ins?

For nail trims, yes, at any location during opening hours. Everything else needs an appointment, though same-day sick slots are held open at every location until 3 PM on weekdays.

How far ahead should I book holiday boarding?

Six to eight weeks for Thanksgiving and the last two weeks of December. Those periods sell out every year and we do not overbook to fit more animals in.

What is your cancellation window?

Free to cancel or move more than 24 hours ahead. Inside 24 hours we charge 50 percent of the service. Holiday boarding needs 7 days. Full detail is in the cancellation and refund policy.

Medical and emergencies

Do you handle emergencies?

We treat urgent but stable cases during opening hours and hold same-day sick slots until 3 PM on weekdays. We are not a 24 hour emergency hospital and we do not have overnight veterinary cover. For anything critical, go straight to the nearest emergency veterinary hospital. For suspected poisoning, call the ASPCA Animal Poison Control Center on (888) 426-4435.

What vaccines do you require?

For boarding, daycare and training classes: dogs need rabies, DHPP and Bordetella, all given at least seven days before arrival. Cats need rabies and FVRCP. We ask for canine influenza where a dog has recently travelled or been in a high density facility. Grooming and veterinary appointments require rabies only.

Can I keep my current veterinarian?

Yes, and plenty of clients do. We will request records with your written permission so our staff know about allergies and medications, and we send visit notes back to your clinic whenever you ask.

Do you offer a second opinion service?

Yes. Bring the records and any imaging, and a veterinarian will review them at a standard exam rate. We will tell you when we agree with the original plan, which is most of the time.

What happens if my pet gets ill while boarding?

We call you first for anything that is not immediately life threatening. If we cannot reach you and the animal needs care, our veterinarian treats to stabilise and we keep calling. Your boarding agreement sets a spending limit you choose in advance, and we do not exceed it without your consent.

Money, billing and insurance

Where can I see your prices?

On the pricing page, in full, including membership plans and every per-service rate. Rates were last reviewed on 1 June 2026 and any change is posted 30 days in advance.

Do you accept pet insurance?

We are not an insurer and we do not sell insurance. We bill you directly and give you an itemised invoice with the codes every major US pet insurer needs for reimbursement. We can submit claim paperwork for Trupanion and Nationwide at no charge.

Do you offer payment plans?

We do not issue credit or offer buy now pay later financing. We accept CareCredit where you already hold an account with them, and we can spread a planned dental or surgical cost across two payments by arrangement before the procedure.

Will I ever get a bill I did not expect?

Any veterinary work over $250 gets a written estimate with a low and a high figure first. If the cost is heading past the high figure we stop and call you. Heavy dematting during a groom is only charged after we have called and you have agreed. Nothing is added without your consent.

Can I cancel a membership?

Yes, with 30 days notice, at any time, with no cancellation fee and no minimum term. You can also pause once per year for up to 60 days.

Daycare, boarding and training

What is the daycare assessment day?

A single paid day where handlers introduce your dog to a small group and watch how they cope. It costs $35 and we refund it if we conclude daycare is not right for your dog. Roughly one dog in eight is better suited to our quiet room than to a group.

My dog is reactive or anxious. Is that a problem?

No, and please tell us in the intake form. We schedule reactive dogs into quiet hours, three of our four sites have a separate entrance, and every member of staff is Fear Free certified. If your dog is not coping we stop, tell you honestly, and change the plan.

What training methods do you use?

Reward based methods only. We do not use prong collars, electronic collars, alpha rolls or anything that works through intimidation, and we will explain the evidence behind that position if you want to hear it. We do not promise a cure or a timeline for any behavior problem.

Can I see my pet while they are boarding?

You get a photo note every day. We do not run public webcams, because the footage tends to worry owners more than it reassures them and it raises real privacy problems for other clients. You can call any time and a handler will tell you how the day is going.

Do you board cats?

Yes, at Denver and Colorado Springs, in a cat wing that is out of sight and sound of the dog areas. Boulder and Fort Collins do not have a cat wing and we would rather say so than put a cat somewhere unsuitable.

Your data

What do you do with my personal information?

We use it to run your bookings, keep your pet's medical record, take payment, and contact you about care. We do not sell it and we do not share it with data brokers. Full detail is in the privacy policy.

How do I get a copy of my pet's records?

Email hello@harborandhound.com from the address on your account and we send them within five business days. There is no charge, including when they go to another clinic.

How do I opt out of the sale or sharing of my information?

We do not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined under the Colorado Privacy Act or the California Consumer Privacy Act. If you would like that confirmed in writing, or want your data deleted, see your rights in the privacy policy.

Can I change my cookie choice?

Yes. Use cookie preferences at any time, or the same link in the footer of every page. Analytics cookies stay off until you accept them.

Still stuck?

Call (720) 431-0688 during opening hours, or send a message and a coordinator will reply within one business day.

Ask us the awkward one

Cost, whether your dog is too difficult, whether you actually need us at all. We would rather answer it now than have you guess.
