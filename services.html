Service 1

Veterinary wellness

Four licensed veterinarians and eleven credentialed technicians across our sites. Routine and preventive medicine, plus urgent but stable cases during opening hours.

  • Annual and semi-annual wellness exams
  • Core and lifestyle vaccines for cats and dogs
  • Dental scaling, polishing and extractions under anaesthesia
  • In-house bloodwork, urinalysis and digital radiography
  • Senior screening panels from age seven
  • Microchipping, spay and neuter, mass removals
Book a visit See veterinary rates
We are not a 24 hour emergency hospital.

If your animal is struggling to breathe, collapsed, bleeding heavily, bloated, seizing, or has swallowed something toxic, do not wait for an appointment here. Go straight to the nearest emergency veterinary hospital, or call the ASPCA Animal Poison Control Center on (888) 426-4435. Nothing on this website is a diagnosis or a treatment plan.

Service 2

Grooming and spa

Seven groomers, two of them National Certified Master Groomers. We groom cats as well as dogs, and we will tell you honestly when a coat needs a shave instead of a brush out.

Standard packages

Bath, blow dry, brush out, nail trim, ear clean, sanitary trim, and a full haircut where the coat calls for one. Priced by size and coat, never by breed guesswork.

Medicated and referral work

Chlorhexidine and antifungal baths on a veterinary instruction, including from your own clinic. Post-surgical coat care coordinated with the treating veterinarian.

Low stress handling

Single-pet appointment slots for nervous animals, no forced restraint, and we stop and call you if a groom is not going well.

Cat grooming

Lion cuts, comb outs, and nail caps by technicians who groom cats every week rather than occasionally.

Service 4

Daycare and enrichment

Groups are capped at twelve dogs and sorted by play style rather than size alone. Every dog does an assessment day before joining a regular group.

Play groups

Rowdy, moderate and gentle groups run in separate rooms with a handler in the room at all times, never watching through glass.

The quiet room

Some dogs hate daycare and that is normal. The quiet room runs enrichment puzzles, scent games and one-to-one handler time for dogs who would rather not be in a group.

Half days, full days and packages

Drop off from 7 AM, pick up until 7 PM on weekdays. Ten day and twenty day packages carry over for twelve months and are shared across pets in the same household.
Service 5

Walking and drop-in visits

The same walker every time wherever the schedule allows, because a dog that knows the person at the door has a better walk. Every visit ends with a written note and a GPS route.

  • Thirty or sixty minute dog walks, solo or paired
  • Cat drop-ins covering litter, food, water and play
  • Medication administration at no extra charge
  • Plant watering and mail collection while you travel
  • Background checked walkers, bonded and insured

Service 6

Behavior and training

Reward based methods only. We do not use prong collars, electronic collars or any method that works by intimidation, and we will explain why if you ask.

What training can and cannot do.

Training changes behavior over weeks and months, and progress depends on the work done between sessions. We do not promise a cure, a timeline, or a specific outcome for any behavior problem, and we will refer to a veterinary behaviorist when a case needs one.

Not sure which service you need?

Bring your pet to the free intro visit and we will tell you honestly, including when the answer is that you do not need us yet.
