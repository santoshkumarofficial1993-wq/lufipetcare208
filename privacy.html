Privacy policy

1. Who we are

Harbor & Hound Pet Care, LLC is a Colorado limited liability company providing veterinary, grooming, boarding, daycare, walking and training services at four locations in Colorado. In this policy, "we", "us" and "our" mean Harbor & Hound Pet Care, LLC. "You" means anyone who uses this website, contacts us, or holds an account with us.

This policy covers www.harborandhound.com and the services we provide in person. It does not cover websites we link to, which have their own policies.

2. What we collect

Information you give us

  • Name, email address, postal address and phone number.
  • Details about your animal: name, species, breed, age, weight, diet, medications, medical history, behavior notes and vaccination records.
  • Emergency contact details and the name of anyone else authorised to collect your pet.
  • Appointment history, service preferences and the notes our staff write after each visit.
  • Payment card details, processed by our payment provider. We do not store full card numbers on our systems.
  • Anything you write to us in a form, an email, a text message or a review.

Information we collect automatically

  • IP address, browser type, device type, operating system and screen size.
  • Pages viewed, the page that referred you, and how long you stayed.
  • Cookie identifiers, as described in section 4.

Information from other people

  • Medical records from a previous veterinary practice, requested only with your written permission.
  • Payment confirmation from our payment processor.
  • Reviews you leave on third party platforms, which are public.

We do not knowingly collect biometric identifiers, precise geolocation from your device, or any special category data about you. We do not buy personal information from data brokers.

4. Cookies and tracking

A cookie is a small file stored on your device. We use two categories.

Strictly necessary cookies

These run the site and cannot be switched off. They remember your cookie choice, keep a form session alive so you do not lose what you typed, and protect forms against automated abuse. They set no advertising identifiers.

Analytics cookies

These count visits and show us which pages people actually read. They are switched off until you accept them through the banner, and nothing in the analytics category loads before that. If you choose "Essential only", no analytics cookie is written at all.

We do not use advertising cookies, retargeting pixels, social media tracking pixels, or cross-site behavioural tracking of any kind on this website.

You can change your choice at any time using the cookie preferences link in the footer of every page. You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings, though strictly necessary cookies being blocked may stop forms working.

Some browsers send a Global Privacy Control signal. We treat that signal as a valid opt out of analytics and of any sale or sharing, and we apply it automatically.

5. Who we share it with

We share personal information only with the following, and only as much as each one needs.

  • Payment processing. Our card processor handles payment data under its own security certification. We receive a confirmation and the last four digits of the card.
  • Practice management and booking software. Hosts your account, appointments and your pet's medical record, on servers located in the United States.
  • Email delivery. Sends confirmations, visit notes and, where you have consented, seasonal reminders.
  • Website analytics. Only if you have accepted analytics cookies.
  • Referral veterinarians and specialist hospitals. Where your pet's care needs it, and with your consent except in a medical emergency.
  • Your own veterinary practice. Where you have asked us to send records.
  • Professional advisers, insurers and auditors. Where necessary and under a duty of confidence.
  • Law enforcement or a regulator. Where the law requires it, or to protect an animal or a person from harm.

Every provider is bound by a written contract that limits them to processing data on our instructions. None of them may use your information for their own marketing.

If the business is ever sold or merged, client information may transfer to the buyer. We would tell affected clients by email before that happened.

6. Selling and sharing

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising, as those terms are defined in the Colorado Privacy Act and the California Consumer Privacy Act. We have not done so in the previous twelve months. We do not sell or share the personal information of anyone under 16 years of age.

Because we do not sell or share, there is nothing for you to opt out of. If you would like that confirmed in writing, email us and we will send it.

7. How long we keep it

  • Veterinary medical records: seven years after the last visit, as Colorado veterinary practice rules require.
  • Non-veterinary service records such as grooming and boarding notes: three years after the last visit.
  • Financial records and invoices: seven years, for tax purposes.
  • Marketing consent records: until you withdraw consent, plus two years to evidence that the consent existed.
  • Website analytics: 14 months.
  • Enquiries that never became a booking: 12 months.

After those periods we delete the information or irreversibly anonymise it.

8. Security

We encrypt data in transit using TLS and at rest in our practice management system. Access is limited to staff who need it for their role, controlled by individual named accounts with multi-factor authentication. Paper records are held in locked storage. We train staff on data handling annually, and we log and review access to medical records.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your personal information and creates a risk to you, we will notify you and the Colorado Attorney General within the time limits set by Colorado law.

We will never ask you for a payment card number, a full social security number, or an account password by email or text message. If you receive a message that appears to be from us asking for those, do not reply, and call us on (720) 431-0688.

9. Your rights

Colorado residents have rights under the Colorado Privacy Act, and California residents have rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended. We extend the same rights to everyone who contacts us, wherever you live.

  • Access. Ask for a copy of the personal information we hold about you.
  • Correction. Ask us to fix anything inaccurate.
  • Deletion. Ask us to delete your information, subject to records we must keep by law.
  • Portability. Receive your data in a portable, machine readable format.
  • Opt out. Of targeted advertising, of any sale, and of profiling. We do none of these, so this right is already satisfied.
  • Withdraw consent. For marketing emails, at any time, using the unsubscribe link or by writing to us.
  • Non-discrimination. We will not refuse service, charge a different price, or reduce service quality because you exercised a right.

To exercise any of these, email hello@harborandhound.com with the word privacy in the subject line, or write to the postal address above. We will verify your identity, usually by confirming details already on your account, and respond within 45 days. If we need longer we will tell you why within that first 45 days and take no more than 45 further days.

You may use an authorised agent to make a request. We will ask for written proof of their authority.

If we refuse a request you may appeal by replying to our decision, and we will respond to the appeal within 45 days. If you remain unhappy, Colorado residents may contact the Colorado Attorney General and California residents may contact the California Privacy Protection Agency.

10. Children

Our services are for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 16. If you believe a child has given us personal information, email us and we will delete it.

11. Changes to this policy

When we make a material change we update the date at the top of this page and email active clients at least 14 days before it takes effect. Minor corrections such as fixing a typo are made without notice. Previous versions are available on request.
