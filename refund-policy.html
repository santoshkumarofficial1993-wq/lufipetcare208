Cancellation and refund policy

Short version: tell us more than 24 hours ahead and you pay nothing. Holiday boarding needs 7 days. If we get something wrong, you do not pay for it.

Cancellation windows No-shows and late arrival Deposits Early collection Memberships Packages and credits When we cancel If you are unhappy How to request a refund Refund timing
Effective date: 1 June 2026 Last updated: 1 June 2026 This policy forms part of our terms of service.

Holiday dates are Thanksgiving week, 20 December to 2 January, and the week containing 4 July. We publish the exact dates each year in September.

To cancel, call (720) 431-0688, reply to your confirmation email, or use the owner portal. The time we receive your message is the time that counts.

2. No-shows and late arrival

A missed appointment with no contact is charged at 100 percent of the service. Arriving more than 15 minutes late usually means we cannot complete the work in the time booked, and we will either shorten it or rebook it and charge the inside-window rate.

Everyone has a bad week. The first no-show on any account is waived automatically, with no need to ask.

3. Deposits

We take a deposit for holiday boarding, equal to one night, and for training courses, equal to 25 percent. No other service requires a deposit. Deposits are applied to the final invoice. Deposits are refundable in full if you cancel outside the window in section 1.

4. Early collection from boarding

Collect early and we refund the unused nights in full, at the nightly rate you were quoted. There is no early departure penalty. Tell us when you can so we can offer the space to someone on the waiting list.

5. Memberships

  • Cancel at any time with 30 days written notice. No cancellation fee and no minimum term.
  • You keep full benefits until the end of the 30 day notice period.
  • We do not refund past months. Membership pays for access during the month, and unused benefits do not roll forward or convert to cash.
  • If you cancel within 14 days of first joining and have used no membership benefit, we refund that first payment in full.
  • You may pause once per year for up to 60 days without losing your rate.
  • If you move out of Colorado or your pet dies, we cancel immediately with no notice period and refund the unused part of the current month.

6. Packages, credits and courses

Daycare packages are valid twelve months from purchase and are shared across pets in one household. Unused days are not refundable in cash after the first 30 days, but they transfer to another pet in your household or to another Harbor & Hound client on request.

Training courses: cancel more than 7 days before the first class for a full refund. Between 7 days and the first class, we refund 50 percent or move you to the next course at no charge, whichever you prefer. Once a course has started we do not refund missed classes, though we will send you the session notes and offer one catch-up slot.

If we assess your dog and conclude that daycare is not right for them, we refund the $35 assessment day in full.

7. When we cancel

Occasionally we have to cancel, usually because a staff member is unwell, a building has a facilities problem, or severe weather makes travel unsafe. In every one of those cases:

  • You pay nothing, and any deposit or prepayment is refunded in full.
  • We offer the next available slot, prioritised ahead of new bookings.
  • For boarding cancelled by us inside 7 days of arrival, we will help you find alternative boarding and cover any difference in nightly rate up to $25 per night.

8. If you are unhappy with a service

Tell us within 7 days. For grooming we will rebook and correct the work at no charge, and if you would rather not come back we refund the groom in full. For any service where we did not do what we agreed to do, we refund it. We do not require you to argue for this.

Veterinary fees are charged for professional time, examination and diagnostics rather than for a guaranteed outcome, so we cannot refund a consultation because the diagnosis was unwelcome. We will always review a bill you think is wrong, and we correct genuine billing errors immediately.

9. How to request a refund

Email hello@harborandhound.com with the word refund in the subject line, or call (720) 431-0688. Include the invoice number if you have it. There is no form to fill in and no fee to request one.

A manager reviews every request and responds in writing within five business days, with the reason if we decline. If you disagree with the decision, section 13 of our terms of service sets out what happens next.

10. Refund timing and method

Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method within five business days of approval. Card refunds typically appear on your statement within a further three to ten business days depending on your bank, which is outside our control. Cash payments are refunded by check or bank transfer, your choice.

We do not charge a processing fee on refunds and we do not issue store credit instead of money unless you ask for it.
