A pet care company built by people who got tired of guessing

Harbor & Hound Pet Care, LLC opened one room on Blake Street in Denver in 2016. Nine years later there are four buildings, 41 credentialed staff, and the same rule about telling owners the truth.

How it started

Priya Raghunathan spent six years in emergency medicine watching the same pattern arrive at 2 AM. A dog with a problem that had been building for months, an owner who had been to four different places, and no single record that connected any of it. The grooming salon did not know about the skin condition. The boarding kennel did not know about the seizure medication. Nobody was wrong, and the animal still suffered for it.

Harbor & Hound was the answer to that: put the veterinary team, the groomers, the daycare handlers and the trainers inside one organisation, on one chart, so information stops falling through the gaps between them.

We are still independently owned. There is no private equity group behind us and no corporate parent setting revenue targets for our veterinarians.

The standards, printed on the wall of every building

These are not aspirations. They are operating limits. When we cannot meet one on a given day, we cut bookings rather than stretch the team, and we call the affected clients ourselves.

Staffing ratios are hard caps

One handler to six dogs in daycare, one to six overnight, and a handler physically in the room rather than watching through glass. When we hit the cap, bookings close for that day. They do not stretch to seven.

Nothing is done by force

Every member of staff is Fear Free certified. If an animal says no clearly enough, we stop, we tell you what happened, and we do not charge for the part we did not complete.

Cleaning on a clock

Play floors between every group rotation, suites twice daily, and a full disinfection cycle overnight. Logs are on a clipboard by the door, and you can ask to see them.

Bad news travels fast

If something goes wrong we call you the same day, before you notice, even when it makes us look careless. Incident reports go out in writing within 24 hours.

No surprise invoices

Written estimates on any veterinary work over $250, a phone call before exceeding the high figure, and no charge for anything you did not agree to first.

The people who will actually see your pet

Six of the 41. Every clinical role holds a current licence or certification, and we pay for continuing education rather than asking staff to fund it themselves.

Working here

Staff turnover in this industry runs high because the pay is low and the days are hard. We pay above the Colorado median for every role, cover continuing education, and cap daycare group sizes partly so handlers can do the job properly rather than survive it.

Open roles are listed with the salary range in the advert. If nothing is open and you still want to talk, write to us.
